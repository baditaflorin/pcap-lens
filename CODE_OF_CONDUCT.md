# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent, but accept responsibility for impact.
- Keep discussion focused on making the project better.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, abuse, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Deliberately derailing technical discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, issues, pull requests, or contributors that violate this code.
