# Privacy

`pcap-lens` v1 runs entirely in your browser.

## What Is Collected

Nothing by the application.

There is no analytics script, tracking beacon, server upload, account system, or capture-storage backend in v1.

## Packet Captures

Packet captures are parsed locally in browser memory. The app does not intentionally upload captures, decoded packets, payloads, rules, or analysis results.

## Local Storage

The app may store small non-sensitive preferences, such as edited IDS rule text. It does not store captures or decoded packet payloads by default.
